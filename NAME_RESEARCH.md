# Name and originality research

Checked: 2026-08-18. This is a practical pre-publication screen, not legal
advice or comprehensive trademark clearance.

## Selected preview name: Relaylings

Why it fits: the small orb helpers relay work to one another, and “-lings” gives
the characters a friendly group identity. Exact-phrase web searches for
`"Relaylings"`, `"Relaylings" AI agents`, `"Relaylings" app`,
`"Relaylings" trademark`, and GitHub results returned no exact product or
software collision in this check.

The name is therefore suitable for a reversible open-source preview. Before a
commercial launch, paid branding, or trademark filing, repeat a professional
clearance search across relevant countries and classes.

Backup options, also with no exact software result in the same preliminary web
check:

1. Deleglow — strongest emphasis on delegation and cheerful visual feedback.
2. Relaylings — selected; clearest character/team connection.
3. Orb Chorus — clearest collaboration metaphor, but more descriptive.

## Rejected codename: Agent Arcade

Do not publish under the old codename. Two active AI businesses already use the
exact phrase: an [autonomous trading platform](https://www.agentarcade.io/) and
an [AI-agent marketplace](https://agentarcade.net/). A related U.S. application
is also publicly listed as serial 99801520; the official lookup surface is the
[USPTO TSDR system](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/). Those facts create unnecessary
confusion even though this project's features and market differ.

## Visual and product originality boundary

Relaylings uses original full-circle SVG orb characters, configurable hues,
four eye styles, soft cheeks, role badges, chatter dots, a winding non-maze
journey, sparks, and generic start/finish symbols. It does not use copied game
character names, pie-slice mouths, maze layouts, pellets, signature sounds,
enemy silhouettes, trade dress, screenshots, or assets from Pac-Man,
Ms. Pac-Man, Grok, or another product. “Maze chase” is inspiration for momentum
and task-clearing only, never a source of protectable expression.
